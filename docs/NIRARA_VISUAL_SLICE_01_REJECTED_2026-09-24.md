# NIRARA — VISUAL SLICE 01

**Status:** REJECTED  
**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Branch:** `visual/slice-01-witness-field-20260924`

The first visual slice is intentionally preserved as R&D evidence but is **not canonical** and must not be used as the base for the next execution.

## Why it was rejected

The slice contained the right nouns but the wrong visual language.

It included:
- watching beings;
- dark forms;
- a crying figure;
- an unresolved centre.

But the scene felt:
- dead;
- flat;
- muted;
- too clean;
- too designed;
- insufficiently painterly;
- insufficiently rich in colour, light and material;
- closer to a dark graphic composition than a living artwork.

The beings also drifted toward simplified figures / mascots rather than living presences.

The central form became too legible as a designed “void” object.

## Canonical lesson

**Darkness is not flatness.**

**Black is not the absence of colour.**

**Reduction is not sterility.**

**Richness is required; filler is forbidden.**

The next visual slice must restart from the reset visual constitution rather than refine this branch.
