# NIRARA — VISUAL SLICE 01 REVIEW

**Branch:** `visual/slice-01-witness-field-20260924`

## Purpose

This slice proves only the canonical foundation:

- small reduced watching beings;
- black structural forms;
- asynchronous blink / gaze;
- isolated crying being in the lower-left;
- an unresolved central presence;
- no decorative filler.

## Implementation

The slice uses no image assets.

The scene is built from HTML, CSS gradients, shape primitives, animation and minimal pointer tracking.

## Deliberate decisions

- The witnesses are not realistic humans.
- Their bodies are reduced and their eyes carry most of the attention.
- The crying being is materially smaller and isolated.
- Its visual cry produces pulses that die into the scene without changing the witnesses.
- The central form is built from overlapping hard/soft layers so it can read as object, cavity, cloth, rock or absence without locking into one.
- Movement hierarchy is restrained: gaze → blink → cry → almost-static centre.
- No unrelated props were added.

## Review questions

Judge the rendered result only on these questions:

1. Do the beings feel alive without becoming characters or mascots?
2. Do the black forms feel like places of hiding / witnessing rather than decorative boxes?
3. Does the lower-left figure feel emotionally isolated without becoming melodramatic?
4. Does the centre resist a stable name?
5. Does the frame feel complete without filler?

If one of these fails, revise the slice. Do not expand scope.
